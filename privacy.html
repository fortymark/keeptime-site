Privacy

Stored on This iPhone

Keep Time stores routines, Recent session summaries, an active-session recovery record, and app preferences on this iPhone. Cue audio is bundled with the app.

Private iCloud Data

Routine data syncs through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available and Keep Time is unlocked. Recent sessions, the active-session recovery record, and app preferences stay local to this iPhone.

Data Not Collected

Keep Time has no third-party analytics, advertising, accounts, subscriptions, or tracking. Apple handles the one-time Keep Time Unlock purchase; the app stores only whether the unlock is active. The app does not collect Health data, contacts, location, routine names, or client information for the developer.

Delete Your Data

Delete individual routines and Recent entries in Library. Deleting Keep Time removes its local data from this iPhone. Delete synced routines in the app to remove them from the private iCloud database through normal iCloud synchronization.

Contacting Support

When you email support@fortymark.com, Forty Mark receives your email address and the information you include to respond to your request. Do not send routine names or client information.

This Website

This website has no analytics or advertising. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
